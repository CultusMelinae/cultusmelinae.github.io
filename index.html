Who we are

Cultus Melinae is a friendly community of around 50 members. We connect through shared interests, creative activities, and music. If you want to contribute, make friends, or just hang out, you're welcome here.

Community

We are always happy to welcome new members. You can start by emailing us, checking our documents, or asking about the band.

Musica Melinae is our band. We rehearse, create music, and perform together. If you play an instrument or want to sing, we’d love to hear from you.
